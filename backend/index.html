    How it works
    CloudArch Advisor lets the AI choose and explain components, while code supplies every number, enforces every rule and writes the infrastructure.
    View source on GitHub
    
  
    
    
    The pipeline
      
      
    1. Intake. Your answers are normalised and length-limited.
      2. 
      
    2. Retrieve. The six most relevant knowledge-base chunks are ranked with BM25 (or Titan embeddings on AWS).
      3. 
      
    3. Generate. The AI proposes three designs as JSON, using only IDs from a 33-service catalog.
      4. 
      
    4. Validate. Any service outside the catalog is rejected and reported.
      5. 
      
    5. Guardrails. Compliance rules add required controls, such as KMS, CloudTrail and GuardDuty for HIPAA.
      6. 
      
    6. Quantify. Monthly cost per user tier and a 1–5 score on each Well-Architected pillar.
      7. 
      
    7. Output. A diagram, a CloudFormation starter and a saved design.

    Scoring
    Each component adds to the pillars it strengthens; for example CloudWatch adds to Operational Excellence and WAF to Security. A pillar score is 1 + 0.75 × the sum, capped at 5. Cost is scored against your budget: 5 at half the budget or less, down to 1 above 150%.
    
    
    Accounts and data
    Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 (120,000 iterations, random salt). Sessions use signed tokens that expire after 7 days. Designs are kept for 30 days.
