# Operational Excellence pillar

Operational Excellence is the ability to run and monitor systems to deliver business value and to continually improve supporting processes.

## Design principles
Perform operations as code, make frequent small reversible changes, refine operations procedures frequently, anticipate failure, and learn from all operational failures.

## Services and practices
Use CloudFormation or the AWS CDK for infrastructure as code. Use CodePipeline, CodeBuild and CodeDeploy for automated delivery. Use CloudWatch metrics, logs, dashboards and alarms for observability, and AWS X-Ray for distributed tracing. Serverless and managed services such as Lambda, DynamoDB and Fargate reduce operational burden, which matters for teams of one to five engineers. Document runbooks and run post-incident reviews.
