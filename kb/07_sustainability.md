# Sustainability pillar

Sustainability focuses on minimising the environmental impact of running cloud workloads.

## Design principles
Understand your impact, establish sustainability goals, maximise utilisation, anticipate and adopt more efficient hardware and software, use managed services, and reduce the downstream impact of your workloads.

## Services and practices
Managed and serverless services such as Lambda, DynamoDB, Fargate and S3 share infrastructure between many customers, which raises utilisation compared with lightly used dedicated servers. Choose Graviton-based instances where possible, scale down when idle, use S3 lifecycle policies to move cold data to cheaper and more efficient storage classes, and avoid over-provisioning. Pick regions with a higher share of renewable energy when latency and data-residency rules allow. The AWS Customer Carbon Footprint Tool reports emissions.
